# Royalty Platform: User Guide

Welcome to the Royalty Platform. This dashboard is designed to eliminate manual spreadsheet math and instantly calculate accurate royalty splits between primary artists and producers based on total streaming revenue.

## 🔄 The Core Workflow

### Step 1: Register a New Track
Before you can calculate payouts, the system needs to know the agreed-upon split percentage for the song.
1. Navigate to the **Add New Track** card.
2. Enter the **Track Title** (e.g., *introduction*).
3. Enter the **Primary Artist** (e.g., *AR3S*).
4. Enter the **Producer Split %** (e.g., *25*).
5. Click **Save Track**. The system will securely save this agreement to the database.

### Step 2: Locate Your Track ID
To calculate a payout, you need the unique system ID for your track.
1. Look at the **Track Catalog** section. 
2. This list automatically refreshes whenever a new track is added.
3. Find your track in the list and note the bolded **ID** (e.g., *ID: 3*).

### Step 3: Calculate the Split Payout
Once your track is in the system and generating streaming revenue, you can calculate exact payouts at any time.
1. Navigate to the **Calculate Split Payout** card.
2. Enter the **Track ID** you found in Step 2.
3. Enter the **Total Revenue** generated across all streaming platforms (e.g., *9000*).
4. Click **Calculate Payout**.
5. The system will retrieve the 25% split agreement from the database, apply it to the $9,000 revenue, and instantly display the exact Producer Payout ($2,250.00).

---
*Note: Split percentages cannot be altered once saved to ensure the integrity of the original producer agreement. If a split changes, register a new version of the track.*
